Capture from your browser

  1. Show the bookmarks bar.
    In Safari, choose View → Show Favorites Bar.
  2. Drag this onto that bar, below the address field.
    Capture to Marginstone
  3. On an article, select a passage and click your new bookmark.
Dragging not working?
  1. Bookmark this page (⌘D) and name it Capture to Marginstone.
  3. Choose Bookmarks → Edit Bookmarks. Replace your new bookmark’s address with what you copied.

Your excerpts are saved in Marginstone. Publishing is separate.
